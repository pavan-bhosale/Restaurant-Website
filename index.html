Chef's Recommendations

Encebollado Ecuatoriano

Our renowned albacore tuna and tender yuca broth infused with cilantro, cumin, and coastal aromatics, topped with tangy pickled red onion curtido and served with chifles.

Heritage & Tradition

Since 2001, the cooking of Ecuador’s coast.

Our encebollado has been celebrated as the best in Europe. Now, we bring that authentic passion to 339 Miracle Mile in Coral Gables.

Founded on deep family traditions, El Ñaño has shared genuine South American hospitality across generations and continents.

Signature Dish 01

Encebollado

A fragrant albacore tuna and yuca broth finished with pickled red onions and fresh cilantro. The iconic bowl that built our international reputation.

Signature Dish 02

Camarones reventados

Crispy golden jumbo prawns served with freshly made patacones, lime wedges, and house dipping sauce. Fresh, crunchy, and irresistible.

Signature Dish 03

Mariscos & Cazuela

Sizzling prawns, mussels, and tender seafood simmered in a coastal garlic-herb reduction with lime and cilantro.

Arroz con maduro y aguacate

Spiced yellow rice accompanied by sweet fried ripe plantains and creamy avocado, served alongside a savory slow-cooked stew.
